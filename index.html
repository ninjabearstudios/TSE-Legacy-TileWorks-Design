About Legacy TileWorks

Old-world standards for modern spaces

Legacy TileWorks & Design was built on a foundation passed down through generations. The name “Legacy” is a tribute to Cameron’s father, who first taught him that the smallest details make the biggest difference. That lesson became the standard that continues today in every project. No shortcuts. No rushed work. Just a consistent commitment to doing things the right way.

Cameron grew up around the tile trade and began learning the craft at an early age. Today, he brings more than 12 years of professional experience serving clients throughout Northern Colorado, the Denver Metro, and Cheyenne areas.

Before the first site visit

Legacy TileWorks specializes in tile services rather than full general-contracting remodels. Cameron handles the tile-related work, including demolition of tile areas, waterproofing, wall replacement or reconstruction where required for the tile system, shower niches, custom benches, curbs, dams, surface preparation, and complete tile installation. Plumbing, electrical, cabinetry, HVAC, and other non-tile trades are handled separately.

Every estimate begins with an on-site consultation. Cameron visits the project, takes measurements, reviews the installation area, discusses the design and specifications, and identifies tile-system preparation or waterproofing requirements. He then prepares a written estimate and delivers it digitally within two business days.
